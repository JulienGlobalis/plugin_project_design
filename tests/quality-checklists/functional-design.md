# Functional Design Checklist

## Current Contract

- [ ] Actors, modules, features, journeys, rules, requirements, and acceptance
      criteria are derived from validated sources.
- [ ] Expected behavior remains independent from technical implementation.
- [ ] Unsupported requirements or acceptance conditions are not invented.
- [ ] Conflicting business rules remain visible until resolved.
- [ ] Functional content is traceable to framing or source material.
- [ ] The output does not prescribe architecture or implementation tasks.
- [ ] The skill remains independently callable and Spec Kit independent.
