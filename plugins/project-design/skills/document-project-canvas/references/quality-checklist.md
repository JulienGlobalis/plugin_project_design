# Document Project Canvas Runtime Quality Checklist

Verify every applicable item before delivery.

## Input and Boundary

- The input is a validated Project Canvas artefact, not raw project sources.
- All ten required Canvas sections are present or explicitly qualified.
- Documentary work has not repaired, enriched, or re-decided business content.

## Content Preservation

- Every material Canvas statement appears once or through an explicit
  cross-reference.
- Meaning, status, lifecycle perspective, authority, and qualification are
  unchanged.
- Contradictions and opposing positions remain visible.
- Stakeholders remain distinct from users.
- MVP, Outside MVP, exclusions, future Options, and Unresolved Scope remain
  distinct when present.
- Decisions remain distinct from Assumptions, Options, preferences, and
  proposals.
- Risks remain distinct from confirmed Issues.
- Questions retain their impact classification and known authority.
- Success criteria contain no invented baseline, measure, or target.
- Readiness qualifications and traceability remain available.

## Documentary Quality

- The title and metadata contain only supplied information.
- Heading hierarchy is complete, sequential, and consistent.
- Tables and registers remain readable and preserve their logical rows.
- Missing information is visible without decorative filler.
- The language and project-specific terminology are preserved correctly.
- A supplied template has not introduced stale content or removed a required
  role.

## Format Verification

- Markdown syntax, headings, tables, links, and fences are valid when used.
- Word output is a real `.docx` and every rendered page has been inspected.
- Google Docs output is native and has been read back after writing.
- The delivered link or file is the verified output.
- Any unsupported or unverified format limitation is stated explicitly.

## Delivery Status

Classify the document result as `PASS`, `PASS WITH RESERVATIONS`, or `FAIL`.
Documentary validation never implies business approval of the Canvas.
