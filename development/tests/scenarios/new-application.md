## Request

```text
Use <selected-skill> to analyze and structure the supplied project material.
Produce the artefact supported by the current skill methodology and make
uncertainty visible.
```

## Applicable Skills

Run this scenario for every specialized skill when its methodology exists. Run

## Expected Observations

- Business objectives, actors, scope, constraints, assumptions, and open
  points are structured without loss.
- MVP content remains distinct from explicitly excluded and future items.
- Unvalidated assumptions do not become decisions or guaranteed capabilities.
- Missing ownership, volume, retention, success, and governance decisions
  remain visible.
- No legacy system, migration constraint, or existing behavior is invented.
- The output stays within the selected skill's responsibility.

## Project Framing-Specific Observations

- Current business handling is described without inventing a legacy
  application.
- Proposed MVP Scope remains provisional until the required authority
  approves it.
- Explicit exclusions remain distinct from future Options.
- Objectives, expected outcomes, target Capabilities, Constraints,
  Requirements, and Business Rules remain distinguishable.
- Product ownership, volume, rollout, waiting lists, approval delegation,
  integrations, retention, support, hosting, success measures, and language
  selection remain unresolved where applicable.
- When French output is requested, terminology is natural and consistent with
  the French companion without translating project-specific Domain Terms
  mechanically.
